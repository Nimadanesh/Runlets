# Template variant: `template-silver`

A third, deliberately different take on the Runlets landing — the "trendy AI
product" edition. Same 13 sections and copy; everything else is new.

## Identity

- **Palette:** silver / chrome / luminous white on near-black `#070709`.
  Liquid-chrome gradient headlines, holographic rims, glass panels.
- **Font:** Unbounded (display) — replaces Space Grotesk. Inter body,
  JetBrains Mono labels stay.
- **Logo:** chrome mark `public/images/runlets-mark-silver.svg` in the
  lockup + favicon.

## Signature component

**The Remix Cube** (`HeroVisual`) — a CSS-3D chrome cube where every face
is a living creation card. Auto-spins; grab and drag to spin it yourself
(with inertia). Saturn orbit ring + floor glow. Resend has its cube;
this one is made of remixes.

## Interactions

Drag-to-spin cube with inertia, coin flips, hover sheen sweeps on frames,
cursor spotlight in hero, scroll-triggered crucible fill, twinkling stars,
flowing pipeline orbs. Reduced-motion fallbacks throughout.
